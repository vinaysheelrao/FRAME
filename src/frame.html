Your folders

Folders are read-only. FRAME never moves, renames, or deletes your videos. Reconnect after reopening if your browser asks for permission. Use Rescan to discover changes.

Metadata & artwork

Lookups send cleaned titles and years to the chosen service. Artwork loads from that service’s image servers. Your video files are never uploaded. IMDb and JustWatch searches open in a new tab; this file does not scrape their pages.

Your library, backed up

Backup includes watchlist, progress, file paths, manual details, and TVmaze data with credits. TMDB-derived details and artwork are excluded; match IDs are retained for refresh. Videos, folder access, and credentials are excluded. Import merges by file identity; select the original folders again to reconnect.

Local playback & storage

Chrome or Edge desktop is recommended for remembered folder handles. The folder upload fallback works without handle persistence. Browser storage is per browser and location; clearing site data removes the saved library. Renaming or moving this HTML may create a separate storage location. MP4 (H.264/AAC) and WebM are the safest formats. MKV, HEVC, AVI, DTS, and other codecs may need an external player. SRT and VTT subtitle files can be loaded in the player. No transcoding or background scanning runs while the page is closed.

A cinema that’s yours.

Bring your collection

Choose one or more folders. FRAME finds videos in subfolders and cleans release tags from filenames. For best matches, use Film Name (2024).mp4 or Series Name S01E02.mkv. Add individual files if folder access is unavailable.

Give every title a face

In Settings, connect TMDB for films and series, or use TVmaze for series without a key. Automatic matching runs after a scan once a metadata source is configured. Use Auto-match library to retry the full library. Strong ranked matches are applied; uncertain matches wait for your choice. Retry includes previously unresolved titles. Open a title to correct its match, add your own details, or search IMDb and JustWatch.

Press play. Settle in.

Play local files with browser controls, resume where you stopped, add subtitles, and keep a watchlist. After reopening, Play will reconnect a permitted folder or ask you to select the video again. Export the library occasionally as a backup.

FRAME is an independent local video library. No films, series, posters, or provider datasets are bundled. The banner uses recently played artwork, then artwork from a recently added title. Without artwork it stays plain.
